# Lexicon: canonical spellings and who says what

Checklists of things that exist, so a candidate can be confirmed as a real name or term and spelled consistently. Ashkenazi form first, Sephardi/Israeli form after the slash where they differ. Spell restored words in the speaker's own style (see `transliteration.md`).

## Who says what: a domain map for attributions

Before accepting a mangled name, ask whether that authority speaks in this domain and makes this claim. Phonetics breaks ties only after that.

| Domain | Authorities you expect | Not expected |
|---|---|---|
| Pshat on Chumash | Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Ramban, Rashbam, Sforno, Radak, Ohr HaChaim, Kli Yakar, Malbim, Netziv, Rav Hirsch | Zohar, Arizal (unless the speaker is giving sod) |
| Pshat on Gemara | Rashi, Tosfos, Rashba, Ritva, Ran, Maharsha, Pnei Yehoshua | Chassidic rebbes |
| Halacha | Rambam, Rif, Rosh, Tur, Shulchan Aruch, Rema, Shach, Taz, Magen Avraham, Mishnah Berurah, Aruch HaShulchan, Ben Ish Chai, Kaf HaChaim, Chazon Ish, Igros Moshe, Minchas Shlomo, Tzitz Eliezer, Yabia Omer, Shevet HaLevi | Rashi on Chumash, Zohar |
| Kabbalah | Zohar (Rashbi), Tikkunei Zohar, Sefer Yetzirah, Ramak (Pardes Rimonim), Arizal and Rav Chaim Vital (Eitz Chaim, Sha'ar HaGilgulim), Ramchal, Shelah, Leshem, Ben Ish Chai, Rashash | Rashi, Rambam (he rarely speaks Kabbalah explicitly) |
| Chassidus | Besht, Maggid, Noam Elimelech, Tanya and Chabad rebbeim, Rebbe Nachman, Sfas Emes, Bnei Yissaschar, Mei HaShiloach, Aish Kodesh | Litvish poskim |
| Mussar | Chovos HaLevavos, Rabbeinu Yonah, Mesilas Yesharim, Orchos Tzaddikim, Rav Yisrael Salanter and the Alters, Rav Dessler, Rav Wolbe, Rav Yerucham | Zohar (as a direct source), Tosfos |
| Machshavah / philosophy | Rav Saadia, Kuzari, Rambam (Moreh), Ramban, Maharal, Rav Hirsch, Rav Kook, Rav Soloveitchik, Rav Dessler, Rav Hutner | Shulchan Aruch |
| Eschatology | Sanhedrin 97–98, Rambam (Hilchos Melachim), Ramban, Ramchal, Kol HaTor, Leshem, Nistaros d'Rashbi, Malbim on Nevi'im | Mishnah Berurah |
| A Gemara's own statement | The Tanna or Amora the Gemara names | A medieval commentator |

Known traps: Rashi on Bereishis 2:7 does not distinguish nefesh from neshamah as soul levels; that teaching is Zohar and Arizal. "The Rav" alone can be Rav Soloveitchik, the speaker's own rav, or an honorific. "The Rebbe" alone depends on the speaker's community. "Rav Shlomo" needs a surname from context.
