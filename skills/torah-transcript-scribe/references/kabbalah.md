# Kabbalah and Chassidus vocabulary

Read when the transcript uses sefirot, worlds, soul levels, partzufim, Zohar Aramaic or Chassidic terms. Standard forms first; Ashkenazi variants after the slash. Capitalize sefirot, partzufim and worlds used as proper nouns ("the sefirah of Chesed", "the world of Atzilut"), lowercase generic uses ("the sefirot", "the worlds"). Italicize Aramaic inline; do not italicize naturalized words (Kabbalah, Zohar, Arizal).

Groupings: Chabad = Chochmah-Binah-Da'at (intellect; also the movement); Chagat = Chesed-Gevurah-Tiferet; Nehi = Netzach-Hod-Yesod; Zeir Anpin = the six from Chesed to Yesod; the right line (Chochmah, Chesed, Netzach), the left line (Binah, Gevurah, Hod), the middle line (Keter, Da'at, Tiferet, Yesod, Malchut). Body map: right arm Chesed, left arm Gevurah, torso Tiferet, right leg Netzach, left leg Hod, brit Yesod, feet or mouth Malchut, brain Chochmah-Binah-Da'at, crown Keter. The sefirot of tohu, shevirat hakeilim, the seven kings of Edom.

## Worlds and soul levels

- Worlds: Adam Kadmon (A"K), Atzilut / Atzilus (emanation), Beriah (creation), Yetzirah (formation), Asiyah (action); abbreviated ABY"A. Heard as "issut", "brea", "yetseerah", "asia".
- Soul levels (NaRaN ChaY): nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, yechidah; also nefesh habahamis / nefesh ha'elokis (Tanya), neshamah yeseirah (Shabbos), tzelem, ohr makif, ohr pnimi.
- Related: gilgul (reincarnation), ibbur (a guest soul), dibbuk, tikkun, nitzotz (spark), niggun, kelipah / kelipot (klipos), kelipat nogah, sitra achra, shevirat hakeilim, birurim, tzimtzum, reshimu, kav, ohr ein sof, ein sof, keilim, orot, ratzo v'shov, mochin, katnus / gadlus, yichud, zivug, hamshachah, hashra'ah, bittul, hitbonenut / hisbonenus, hitbodedut / hisbodedus, devekut / devekus, kavanot / kavanos, yichudim.

## Partzufim and angels

- Partzufim (Arizal): Atik Yomin / Atika Kadisha, Arich Anpin (A"A), Abba, Imma, Zeir Anpin (Z"A), Nukva; Yisrael Saba and Tevunah; Yaakov and Rachel; Leah.
- Angels: malachim, ofanim, serafim, chayot hakodesh, keruvim, ishim, erelim, chashmalim; Michael, Gavriel, Uriel, Refael, Metatron (also called *Na'ar*, identified with Chanoch), Sandalfon (Eliyahu), Samael, Lilith, Ashmedai, the satan, the malach hamaves. Heard as: "metal tron", "sandal phone", "summer elves".

## Texts and authors

Sefer Yetzirah; Sefer HaBahir; Zohar (Rashbi; Idra Rabba, Idra Zuta, Sifra d'Tzniusa, Ra'aya Mehemna, Zohar Chadash, Tikkunei Zohar with Patach Eliyahu); Sha'arei Orah (Rav Yosef Gikatilla); Pardes Rimonim and Tomer Devorah (Ramak, Rav Moshe Cordovero); the Arizal (Rav Yitzchak Luria) through Rav Chaim Vital: Eitz Chaim, Pri Eitz Chaim, Sha'ar HaKavanos, Sha'ar HaGilgulim, Sha'arei Kedushah, Shemonah She'arim; Rama MiFano (Asarah Ma'amaros); Shelah HaKadosh; Ramchal (Kelach Pischei Chochmah, Da'as Tevunos, Derech Hashem); Rashash (Rav Shalom Sharabi, Nahar Shalom); Leshem (Rav Shlomo Elyashiv); Rav Yitzchak Ginsburgh; Rav Aryeh Kaplan (translations). Ba'al Shem Tov, Maggid of Mezritch, Tanya, Likutei Torah, Likutei Moharan, Sfas Emes, Mei HaShiloach and other Chassidic works carry this vocabulary into Chassidus.
