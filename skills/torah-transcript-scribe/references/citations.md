# Citations: how sources are spoken, and how to write them

Speakers cite sources out loud in Hebrew letter-numbers and genre-specific words. Engines turn these into syllables ("Reish Yud"), English words ("cough" for kuf) or nothing. Use this file to decode what was said and to check whether the cited place exists.

## Hebrew letters as numbers

| Letter | Value | Heard as |
|---|---|---|
| alef | 1 | olive, a leaf |
| beis / bet | 2 | base, bait, bet |
| gimmel | 3 | gimbal, gimble |
| dalet / daled | 4 | dollar, Dallas |
| hei | 5 | hey, hay |
| vav | 6 | vov, wow |
| zayin | 7 | design, Zion |
| ches / chet | 8 | chess, het |
| tes / tet | 9 | test, tet |
| yud | 10 | you'd, yood, Jude |
| kaf / chaf | 20 | cough, calf |
| lamed | 30 | lame, lamb |
| mem | 40 | mem, meme |
| nun | 50 | noon, none |
| samech | 60 | summit, sam ech |
| ayin | 70 | eye in, I in, ain |
| pei / fei | 80 | pay, fay |
| tzadi | 90 | sadie, tzaddik |
| kuf | 100 | cough, koof |
| reish | 200 | rage, raysh |
| shin | 300 | shin, sheen |
| tav / taf | 400 | tough, tav |

Compounds add: yud-zayin = 17, chaf-beis = 22, ayin-beis = 72, kuf-lamed-dalet = 134, reish-yud = 210. 15 is tes-vav and 16 is tes-zayin (never yud-hei, yud-vav). 500 and up: tav-kuf (500), tav-reish (600), tav-shin (700), tav-tav (800). Write the numeral when the letters function as a reference (siman 210, daf 17a); keep the letter names when the letters are the subject (gematria, acrostics, the "letter mem").

## Gemara

- Form: masechta, daf, amud: "Berachos daf yud-zayin amud alef" = Berachos 17a; "Sanhedrin tzadi-zayin" = Sanhedrin 97. "Amud beis" = b. Yerushalmi is cited by perek and halacha; Mishnah by perek and mishnah ("Avos perek beis mishnah daled" = Avos 2:4).
- Words: daf (page), amud (side), perek (chapter), mishnah, Tosfos d"h (dibbur hamaschil, the opening words), Rashi d"h, sugya, Bavli, Yerushalmi, Tosefta, braisa, memra, Rishonim al hadaf.
- Aramaic frames that garble: tanu rabbanan (the Rabbis taught), amar Rav, amar Rabbi Yochanan, ika d'amri (some say), lishna achrina (another version), tanya, mesayim (it concludes), kashya (a difficulty), teiku, mai nafka minah, hachi nami, ela, iy hachi.
- Last daf of each Bavli masechta, for checking that a cited daf exists: Berachos 64, Shabbos 157, Eruvin 105, Pesachim 121, Shekalim 22, Yoma 88, Sukkah 56, Beitzah 40, Rosh Hashanah 35, Ta'anis 31, Megillah 32, Moed Katan 29, Chagigah 27, Yevamos 122, Kesubos 112, Nedarim 91, Nazir 66, Sotah 49, Gittin 90, Kiddushin 82, Bava Kamma 119, Bava Metzia 119, Bava Basra 176, Sanhedrin 113, Makkos 24, Shevuos 49, Avodah Zarah 76, Horayos 14, Zevachim 120, Menachos 110, Chullin 142, Bechoros 61, Arachin 34, Temurah 34, Kerisos 28, Me'ilah 22, Niddah 73. A "daf" past the end of the masechta is a mishearing of the number or the masechta.
- Frequently cited pages worth recognizing: Berachos 17a (Rav's prayer), 55a–57b (dreams), Shabbos 31a (Hillel and the convert), 88a (na'aseh v'nishma), 118b, Pesachim 94a, Yoma 85b–86a (teshuva), Sukkah 52a (yetzer hara), Rosh Hashanah 16b–17a, Megillah 7b, 14a (seven prophetesses), Chagigah 12a–14b (ma'aseh merkavah, Pardes), Yevamos 62b–63a, Kesubos 111b, Sotah 22b (seven types of perushim), 49b (ikvesa d'meshicha), Gittin 55b–56b (destruction), Kiddushin 30b–31a, 40b, Bava Metzia 59b (tanur shel Achnai), 85a, Bava Basra 14b–15a (Tanach order), Sanhedrin 37a, 90a–92b (techiyas hameisim), 97a–99a (Mashiach), Makkos 23b–24a, Avodah Zarah 3b, 17a, Menachos 29b, 43b, Chullin 60b, Niddah 30b–31a (the fetus), Avos 1:1, 2:4, 5:21.

## Rambam and the codes

- Rambam: "Hilchos Teshuvah perek gimmel halacha daled" = Hilchos Teshuvah 3:4. The 14 books are in `lexicon.md`. Moreh Nevuchim is cited by chelek and perek (2:32). Peirush HaMishnayos by masechta; Shemoneh Perakim; Igeres Teiman; Igeres HaShmad.
- Tur and Shulchan Aruch: four chalakim, Orach Chaim (OC, daily life and the year), Yoreh De'ah (YD, kashrus, niddah, aveilus, ribbis, Torah study), Even HaEzer (EH, marriage), Choshen Mishpat (CM, civil law). Form: "Orach Chaim siman kuf-lamed-daled se'if beis" = OC 134:2. Mishnah Berurah by siman and sif katan ("sif katan yud" = s.k. 10). Shach and Taz on YD and CM, Magen Avraham and Taz on OC, Beis Shmuel and Chelkas Mechokek on EH, Sma and Ketzos on CM, Pischei Teshuvah on YD, EH and CM, Beis Yosef and Darkei Moshe and Bach on the Tur.
- Ben Ish Chai is cited by year, parsha and halacha: "Shanah Rishonah, Parashat Va'era, halacha daled". Kaf HaChaim by siman and os (letter). Kitzur Shulchan Aruch by siman and se'if. Aruch HaShulchan by siman and se'if. Yalkut Yosef by volume and siman. Chayei Adam by klal and se'if.

## Responsa (shu"t, she'eilos u'teshuvos)

The word "teshuva" (a responsum) garbles into "shiva", "chuva", "to shoe va". "Shu"t" garbles into "shoot". Form: "Igros Moshe Orach Chaim chelek daled siman kuf" = Igros Moshe OC 4:100. "Mahadura Kama / Tinyana" means first or second edition (Noda B'Yehuda, Sho'el U'Meishiv).

Often cited by name: Terumas HaDeshen; Maharik; Mahari Weil; Radbaz; Maharshal; Rema; Maharam Padua; Maharam Lublin; Chavos Yair; Shevus Yaakov; Noda B'Yehuda (Rav Yechezkel Landau, Kama and Tinyana); Chasam Sofer (Rav Moshe Sofer); Ksav Sofer; Rabbi Akiva Eiger; Shoel U'Meishiv; Maharam Schick; Binyan Tzion (Rav Yaakov Ettlinger); Beis Yitzchak; Maharsham; Avnei Nezer; Divrei Chaim; Melamed L'Ho'il (Rav David Tzvi Hoffmann); Achiezer (Rav Chaim Ozer); Seridei Eish (Rav Yechiel Yaakov Weinberg); Chelkas Yaakov; Har Tzvi (Rav Tzvi Pesach Frank); Mishpetei Uziel; Igros Moshe (Rav Moshe Feinstein); Minchas Yitzchak (Rav Yitzchak Yaakov Weiss); Minchas Shlomo (Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach); Tzitz Eliezer (Rav Eliezer Waldenberg, 22 volumes, medical halacha); Shevet HaLevi (Rav Shmuel Wosner); Shevet miYehuda (Rav Isser Yehuda Unterman); Yabia Omer and Yechaveh Daas (Rav Ovadia Yosef); Rav Pe'alim and Torah Lishmah (Ben Ish Chai); Be'er Moshe (Rav Moshe Stern); Mishneh Halachos (Rav Menashe Klein); Divrei Yatziv (Klausenberger Rebbe); Teshuvos v'Hanhagos (Rav Moshe Shternbuch); Shevet HaKehasi; Minchas Asher (Rav Asher Weiss).

## Tanach and Midrash

- Form: sefer, perek, pasuk: "Bereishis perek beis pasuk zayin" = Bereishis 2:7. Speakers also cite by parsha ("in Parshas Vayeira") or by event ("by the Akeidah"). Tehillim by "kapitel" (Yiddish) or perek: "kapitel kuf-lamed" = Tehillim 130.
- Midrash: "Bereishis Rabbah parsha yud-beis" (12), Tanchuma by parsha, Yalkut Shimoni by remez number ("Yalkut Shimoni remez tav-tzadi-tes" = 499), Pirkei d'Rabbi Eliezer by perek, Tanna d'Vei Eliyahu by perek.
- Targumim: Onkelos, Yonasan ben Uziel, Yerushalmi. Rashi on Chumash is cited by pasuk and dibbur hamaschil.

## Checking a citation

1. Does the sefer exist and is it spelled the way its author is known? (`lexicon.md`)
2. Does the sefer have that unit: a 23rd chelek of a 22-volume set, a daf past the masechta's end, a siman in Orach Chaim above 697 or in Yoreh De'ah above 403, a mishnah number past the perek's length, are all mishearings of the number or the work.
3. Does the cited place plausibly discuss the topic? If you know the sugya, confirm; if a Sefaria tool is available, look it up; if neither, write the citation as heard and note "citation numbers as heard; verify".
4. Never replace an unverifiable citation with a better-known one. A wrong but famous source is a worse error than a garbled number.

## Looking a citation up on Sefaria

When a Sefaria tool is available, every citation and quoted line gets a lookup, and the review records one of three outcomes: **verified** (the place exists and says what the speaker says), **exists, topic unverified**, or **not resolvable**. Order of attempts:

1. Ask the catalogue tool for the work by its Sefaria English title before fetching text. Sefaria's spellings differ from the shiur's: Berakhot, Shabbat, Bava Metzia, Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim, Yoreh De'ah, Even HaEzer, Choshen Mishpat, Mishneh Torah, Mishnah Berurah, Rashi on Genesis, Zohar. The catalogue answer gives the unit names (Siman and Seif, Daf and Line, Chapter and Verse) and the section counts.
2. Fetch the text at the unit the speaker named ("Berakhot 17a", "Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 271:1") and compare it with the speaker's translation or paraphrase. Agreement is **verified**. A real place whose content you cannot match is **exists, topic unverified**.
3. A title the catalogue does not know, a unit past the work's length, a lookup error, or a responsa collection that is not on Sefaria is **not resolvable**. Write the citation as heard and say so. Not resolvable never means the speaker was wrong.
4. Do not "verify" by text search alone. A Hebrew search for a sefer's name finds the pasuk or phrase the sefer is named after (searching for the Noda B'Yehuda returns Tehillim 76:2), and a search hit in the wrong work proves nothing about the citation.

Section counts that catch a misheard number (verified on Sefaria's catalogue): Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim 697 simanim, Yoreh De'ah 403, Even HaEzer 178, Choshen Mishpat 427; Mishnah Berurah follows Orach Chaim; Tehillim 150 perakim; Mishlei 31; Iyov 42; Bereishis 50 perakim, Shemos 40, Vayikra 27, Bamidbar 36, Devarim 34; Avos 6 perakim; Hilchos Teshuvah 10 perakim, Hilchos Shabbos 30, Hilchos Dei'os 7, Hilchos Yesodei HaTorah 10. A number past the end is a misheard number or a misheard work, never a reason to pick a different famous source.
